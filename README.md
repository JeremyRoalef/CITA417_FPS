<h1>Overview</h1>
This lab aims to develop an objective-based system for player progression. The player moves through the world with their objective updating as they progress.

For this lab, I developed a simple objective state handler, where the player's objective changes as they play the game. They start with the objective to clear the area. Then, they are prompted to head to the next section and clear that area. After progressing a couple of times, they are prompted to head to extraction, where their objectives are complete.
